2. Definition of Terms

2.1. Personal data (hereinafter – "data"): Any information related to an identified or identifiable natural person. 2.2. Biometric data: Physical, mental, or behavioral characteristics unique and permanent to each natural person by which they can be identified (e.g., facial characteristics, fingerprints, retinal image).

2.3. Data processing: Any action performed on data using automatic, semi-automatic, or non-automatic means, such as collection, recording, photographing, audio recording, video recording, organizing, storing, changing, restoring, retrieving, using, disclosing, transmitting, distributing, grouping, combining, blocking, erasing, or destroying.

2.4. Data subject (hereinafter – "user"): A natural or legal person using the Bankysh technology platform, about whom data is processed.

2.5. Consent: The voluntary consent expressed by the user (data subject) to the processing of data for a specified purpose after receiving relevant information orally, electronically, or by appropriate communication means.

2.6. Data processor: For the present purposes – Bankysh LLC (and/or Partner Payment Providers acting as independent controllers/processors) who determines the purposes and means of personal data processing directly or through an authorized person.

2.7. Authorized person: An employee or designated sub-contractor of Bankysh who processes data for or on behalf of the data processor.

2.8. Third party: Any natural or legal person, public institution, other than the data subject, Personal Data Protection Service, data processor, or authorized person.
